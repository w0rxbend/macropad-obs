# The critical copper route.py draws and locks before any other routing (task 5.8), first line first.
# A line is a net, its pads, then options. Two or more pads are joined into one group of copper; a
# single pad gets a via to its net's plane. `wide` asks for a wide track where one fits; `no-via` keeps
# the copper on its pads' layer. The four ground vias in the module's pad 41 come before line 1.
#
# Buck loop (TLV62569, SLVSDG1C section 10): the input capacitor to VIN and to GND, the switch node, the
# output capacitor, the feedback divider, then the vias into the planes. EN sits on +5V through its via.
+5V      C1.1 U2.4        wide
GND      C1.2 U2.2        wide
BUCK_SW  U2.3 L1.1        wide
+3V3     L1.2 C2.1        wide
BUCK_FB  U2.5 R7.2 R8.1
GND      C1.2
GND      C2.2
GND      R8.2
+5V      C1.1
+5V      U2.1
+5V      F1.2
+3V3     C2.1
+3V3     R7.1
# Module: 22 uF and 100 nF on the 3V3 pad, the ground pads beside the antenna into the plane.
+3V3     U1.2 C3.1 C4.1   wide
+3V3     C3.1
GND      C3.2
GND      C4.2
GND      U1.1
GND      U1.40
# USB pair on B.Cu only, over the unbroken ground of In2.Cu (report 10 section 10): jack, ESD, 22 ohm,
# module. The ESD ground and the jack's ground pads go first, VBUS after the pair. D+ goes before D-:
# it is the inner line round the module, and D- first would wall it in under the ESD part.
GND      U3.2
GND      J1.A1
GND      J1.A12
USB_D_P  J1.A6 J1.B6 U3.4 U3.3 R6.1   no-via
USB_D_N  J1.B7 J1.A7 U3.6 U3.1 R5.1   no-via
USB_DM   R5.2 U1.13   no-via
USB_DP   R6.2 U1.14   no-via
# CC2 before VBUS: jack pad B5 sits between the pair and VBUS, over the ESD part, and the bus over the
# pad row would wall it in; Freerouting finds no way out of it then (task 5.9).
USB_CC2  J1.B5 R2.1 R4.1
VBUS     J1.A4 J1.B9 J1.A9 J1.B4 U3.5 F1.1
